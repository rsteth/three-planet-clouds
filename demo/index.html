About the demo

Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom. In Ant view, drag to look around. System clouds start hidden. High stays on Low while its worker prepares the textures, or if required GPU features are unavailable.

Weather combines hierarchical Low shapes with continuous High density. Legacy selects the original ant-world model for comparison. The two qualities share weather patterns; their detailed silhouettes differ.
